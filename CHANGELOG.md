# Changelog

One line per version: date, version, what changed in plain words.

- 2026-11-16 · 0.5.2 · Travelers: add names to a trip; the packing list then has tabs Visi · Bendri · each person, with their own items, templates and progress. Removing a traveler moves their items to Bendri.
- 2026-11-16 · 0.5.1 · Packing templates: Būtiniausi, Paplūdimys, Miestas, Žiema (socks and underwear per trip day, up to ×7), your own templates (save, rename, delete), no duplicates, Undo. Duplicating a trip copies its packing list unticked.
- 2026-11-16 · 0.5.0 · Packing list: add items by category, tick them off, quantity ×N, hide packed, start over (with Undo); progress on the trip screen. Travels in the trip file.
- 2026-11-16 · 0.4.2 · Backup reminder: a slim strip on the trip screen when bookings or tickets changed since the last export; Eksportuoti or ✕ (hidden until the next change). Not shown for finished or archived trips.
- 2026-11-16 · 0.4.1 · Import a trip file: Importuoti kelionę on Home checks the file, shows a summary and adds the trip (or a copy, or replaces the one on the phone); all or nothing. App updates no longer download PDF.js again.
- 2026-11-16 · 0.4.0 · Export a trip: ⋯ → Eksportuoti kelionę makes one .lagio file with the trip, its bookings and all tickets, to share or keep as a backup. Works offline.
- 2026-11-16 · 0.3.3 · Tickets on bookings: attach PDFs and photos to a booking, see 📎 counts in the list, move a document between bookings and "Kiti dokumentai" (with Undo).
- 2026-11-16 · 0.3.2 · PDFs now show every page inside the app with pinch and +/− zoom (PDF.js, works offline). Flights, trains, buses and ferries may arrive "before" they leave when crossing time zones (a note instead of an error); date errors use the field names. Space of deleted files is freed after 7 days.
- 2026-11-16 · 0.3.1 · Bookings: add flights, trains, stays and more with times, code, address, price and note; list grouped by day; copy the code, open the address in maps, edit and delete (with Undo).
- 2026-11-16 · 0.3.0 · Documents: add PDFs and photos to a trip, view them full screen, share, download or delete them (with Undo). Everything opens offline.
- 2026-11-16 · 0.2.1 · Trip form: start and end date stay level when an error shows. Duplicating a trip leaves the copy without dates.
- 2026-11-16 · 0.2.0 · Trips: create, edit, duplicate, archive and delete trips (with Undo); trip list grouped by now / no dates / past; each trip has a dashboard with a countdown or "day 3 of 7".
- 2026-11-16 · 0.1.0 · First version: app shell in Lithuanian and English, empty trip list, works offline and can be installed.
